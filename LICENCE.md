# Licence

This repository contains datasets used in the Brunel Centre Data Hub.

Datasets are derived from a range of sources, including UK government departments, public bodies, and research outputs produced by the Brunel Centre or partner organisations.

Where datasets are derived from UK public sector information, the data are generally available under the terms of the Open Government Licence v3.0 (OGL).

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Some datasets may be subject to licensing terms other than the Open Government Licence v3.0, including research data licences or third-party licensing conditions. Where this is the case, the applicable licence will be documented in the dataset metadata or accompanying documentation.

Users are responsible for complying with the licence conditions specified by the original data providers, including providing appropriate attribution when reusing the data.

The Brunel Centre does not claim ownership of underlying third-party data sources unless explicitly stated.
